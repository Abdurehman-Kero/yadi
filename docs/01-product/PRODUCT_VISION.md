# Product Vision

## Purpose
Yadi lets people organize their lives by talking naturally. It turns statements like "assignment due Friday, meeting tomorrow at 10, two hours on my project, exercise in the evening" into structured tasks, a feasible schedule, and dependable reminders, and it lets the user correct the plan conversationally.

## Target users (initial)
Students and busy professionals who juggle deadlines and fixed commitments and want planning to take less effort. (Personas to be written in `USER_PERSONAS.md`.)

## Problems solved
- Manually entering and prioritizing every task is tedious.
- To-do lists ignore available time; calendars ignore flexible work.
- Chatbots give advice but do not change real data or follow through.

## Goals
1. Natural-language capture with transparent, editable results.
2. Realistic schedules produced by a deterministic engine.
3. Reminders that work when the app is closed.
4. User control: review, correct, approve, cancel.
5. Privacy-respecting, inspectable memory (later).

## Non-goals
Not just a to-do list, calendar, advice chatbot, notification app, or an autonomous agent acting without permission.

## Principles
Simple interaction · user control · realistic planning · reliable follow-through · useful but controlled memory · explainable decisions · graceful recovery · progressive intelligence.

## Long-term direction
Calendar sync, voice-first use, proactive briefings and risk warnings, mobile and possibly wearables — all built on a core that is independent of any device.

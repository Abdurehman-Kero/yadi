# User Personas

## Layla — university student
Deadlines, classes, group projects; underestimates how long work takes; wants "what should I do next?" and gentle reminders. Pain: planning overhead, missed deadlines.

## Omar — working professional
Meetings fixed by others, many small tasks, limited focus time. Wants realistic day plans, conflict warnings, quick capture on the go (voice later).

## Sara — busy parent / self-organiser (secondary)
Recurring routines, interruptions. Wants simple rescheduling by talking ("I couldn't finish today, rearrange tomorrow").

Common needs: low-effort capture, trustworthy reminders, control over AI decisions, privacy.
